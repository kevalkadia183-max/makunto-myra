About Makunto

Makunto is a voice-first AI companion for content creators. Myra, your AI companion, tracks your social media channels, explains your analytics out loud in plain language, watches competitors and trends, and coaches you on growing your audience — all through natural voice conversation.

Makunto currently supports YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, with more platforms on the way.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you sign up (directly or through Google sign-in).
  • Connected platform data — when you connect a social account (e.g. YouTube), we access channel statistics, video analytics and related metadata through the platform's official API, only with your explicit authorization.
  • Voice input — what you say to Myra is transcribed to text to generate a response. Audio is processed transiently and is not stored as a recording.
  • Conversation history — your text conversations with Myra are stored so she can remember context across sessions. You can clear them at any time.
  • Technical data — basic diagnostics (errors, device type) used solely to keep the service working.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not post to, modify or delete anything on your connected social accounts.
  • We do not use your private analytics to train AI models.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except the service providers strictly required to operate the product (AI processing, voice synthesis, authentication, hosting).

Google user data

Makunto's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed read-only, displayed to you, and used only to power the analytics and coaching features you see in the app. You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

AI Usage Policy

  • What the AI does. Myra uses large language models to interpret your questions, analyze your channel statistics, and speak responses using text-to-speech.
  • Human oversight. AI output is assistive. Verify important numbers in your platform's official dashboard before acting on them.
  • No training on your data. Your private analytics and conversations are used only to serve your session — they are not used to train models.
  • Transparency. You are always talking to an AI. Myra will never pretend to be a human.

Data Retention Policy

  • Conversation history — kept until you clear it in the app or delete your account.
  • Analytics snapshots — recent snapshots of connected-channel statistics are kept to compute day-over-day changes; older snapshots are periodically pruned.
  • Voice audio — processed in real time; recordings are not retained after transcription.
  • Account data — retained while your account is active and deleted upon account deletion (see below).

Account Deletion Policy

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting support from your account email. Deletion includes your profile, conversation history, connected-account records and analytics snapshots. Deletion is permanent and completed within 30 days of the request. Disconnecting a social platform (without deleting your account) immediately stops new data collection for that platform.

Copyright & DMCA Policy

If you believe content surfaced through the service infringes your copyright, send a notice to support including: identification of the work, the allegedly infringing material, your contact information, a good-faith statement, and your physical or electronic signature. Security & Privacy Overview

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Sign-in is handled by a dedicated authentication provider (Clerk) — Makunto never sees or stores your password.
  • Social platforms are connected via OAuth — Makunto never asks for your platform passwords, and access is read-only.
  • Server-side authorization checks protect every personal-data endpoint; anonymous visitors cannot access anyone's data.
  • You can revoke Makunto's platform access at any time from the platform's own security settings.

Found a vulnerability? Please report it responsibly via support — we take reports seriously and respond quickly.

Accessibility Statement

Makunto is designed to be usable by everyone. Being voice-first, the core experience works without reading the screen. We also provide: adjustable speech rate and pitch, high-contrast dark theme, large touch targets, reduced-motion support honoring your device setting, and text transcripts of every conversation in the Log tab. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please tell support — fixing it becomes a priority.

FAQ

Is Makunto free?

Makunto is currently in early access. Pricing for premium features will be announced in the app before anything changes.

Which platforms can I connect?

YouTube, Instagram and Facebook today. X (Twitter), TikTok, LinkedIn, Threads and Pinterest are coming soon.

Can Myra post or change anything on my accounts?

No. Access is strictly read-only analytics. Myra can never post, edit or delete anything.

Why does Myra need microphone access?

Only to hear your questions. Audio is transcribed in real time and not stored as a recording.

My numbers look different from my platform dashboard — why?

Platform APIs update on a delay (YouTube analytics can lag up to 48 hours). Myra always tells you the freshest data the API provides.

How do I disconnect a platform or delete my data?

Disconnect any platform from the Social Accounts page. For full account deletion, see the Account Deletion Policy.
