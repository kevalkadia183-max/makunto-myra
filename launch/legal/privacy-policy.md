# Makunto Privacy Policy (website version)

> Canonical, always-current version lives in the app at `/legal.html`. Keep both in sync. Last updated: August 2026.

**Makunto Myra** ("Makunto", "we") is a voice-first AI assistant for content creators. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## What we collect
- **Account info** — name and email from your sign-in provider (Clerk authentication).
- **Connected platform data** — when you connect YouTube, Instagram or Facebook via official OAuth, we access channel statistics, video/post metrics and (for your own YouTube channel) private analytics such as watch time and traffic sources. Access is **read-only**.
- **Conversations** — your chats with Myra are saved to your account so she can remember context. Voice audio is processed to text for answering and not retained as recordings.
- **Usage & diagnostics** — basic technical logs (errors, device type) to keep the service working.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not post, edit or delete anything on your connected accounts.
- We do not use your private analytics to train public AI models.
- We do not share your data with advertisers.

## Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure
Makunto's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide user-facing features (your analytics, briefs and Myra's answers), is never sold, never used for advertising, and never read by humans except with your consent, for security, or as required by law.

## How data is used
Your data powers your own features: live answers, the daily brief, competitor comparisons, and conversation memory. AI processing (speech-to-text, chat, text-to-speech) is performed by vetted providers under data-processing agreements; only the minimum needed content is sent.

## Retention & deletion
- Data is kept while your account is active.
- Disconnecting a platform stops collection immediately.
- You can delete conversations, disconnect accounts, or delete your entire account and all associated data — from Settings or by emailing `support@makunto.app` (placeholder — replace with live inbox).
- Deletion requests are honored within 30 days.

## Security
All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). OAuth tokens are stored server-side and never exposed to your browser. Access to production data is restricted and logged.

## Your rights (GDPR/CCPA)
You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data at any time. EU/UK users may also lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority.

## Children
Makunto is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We'll update this page and note the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
