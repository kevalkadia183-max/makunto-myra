# Marketing Asset Checklist & Specs

**Style guide for every asset:** background #07070D with subtle grid texture · purple glow #8B5CF6 → blue #3B82F6 gradient accents · Sora (headings) + Inter (body) · glassmorphism cards · the Myra orb as the hero device.
