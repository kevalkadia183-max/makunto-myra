Questions, answered

What is Makunto Myra?

A voice-first AI copilot for creators. She connects to your social accounts, watches your numbers, and answers questions out loud — with reasons and next steps.

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube (including private analytics), Instagram and Facebook today. X, TikTok, LinkedIn, Threads and Pinterest are coming soon.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Official OAuth connections (never your passwords), read-only access, encrypted in transit, never sold. Disconnect or delete anytime.

Does Myra post to my accounts?

No — Myra is read-only. She analyzes and advises; creating and publishing is what Makunto Studio (coming soon) is for.

Do I need a big channel?

No. If you're starting out, Myra becomes a mentor: niche picking, channel naming, first videos, posting habits.

How much does it cost?

There's a free tier to explore. Personal analytics and premium voices are part of paid plans — see Pricing.
