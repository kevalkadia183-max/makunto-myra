## Boilerplate (short)
Makunto Myra is a voice-first AI copilot that turns creator analytics into conversation. Instead of decoding dashboards, creators simply ask — "how's my channel doing?" — and Myra answers out loud with live numbers, the reasons behind every change, and what to do next.

## Boilerplate (long)
Makunto builds AI copilots for content creators. Its first product, Myra, connects to a creator's YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts and replaces dashboard-digging with spoken conversation. Myra greets creators with what changed since they last talked, delivers a seven-part daily action brief, tracks competitors, and connects platform trends to each creator's niche — all grounded in live data and delivered in natural speech. Myra is read-only and privacy-first: official OAuth connections, no data selling, delete anytime. A companion product, Makunto Studio, focused on content creation and publishing, is in development.
