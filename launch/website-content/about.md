## Short version (footer / meta)
Makunto builds AI copilots for content creators. Our first product, Myra, turns your channel analytics into a spoken conversation — what changed, why, and what to do next.

## About page copy

### The problem
Every creator has the same two tabs open: their content, and a wall of analytics they don't have time to decode. Dashboards show *what* happened. They never explain *why*, and they never tell you what to do about it.

### Our answer
Makunto Myra is a growth copilot you talk to. Connect your YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, then just ask: "How's my channel doing?" "Why did views drop?" "What should I post this week?" Myra answers out loud, grounded in your live numbers — with the reason behind every change and a concrete next step.

She greets you with what changed since you last spoke, builds a seven-part action brief every morning, watches your competitors, and connects trends to your niche. It's the experience of having a sharp growth strategist on call — except she's available at 7am in your kitchen, hands-free.

### What we believe
- **Answers, not charts.** Data is only useful when it ends in a decision.
- **Voice is the interface.** Creators are busy making things; talking is faster than digging.
- **Your data is yours.** Official OAuth connections, read-only access, no selling data, delete anytime.

### The Makunto family
- **Makunto Myra** — the AI creator companion: analytics, monitoring, coaching, strategy. *(Available now)*
- **Makunto Studio** — creating, editing and publishing content. *(Coming soon)*

## Support page content

**Help & Support**

1. **In-app help** — Settings → Help & FAQ covers setup, voices, connecting accounts and troubleshooting.
2. **Common fixes**
   - *Myra can't hear me:* check mic permission in your browser/phone settings, then reload.
   - *No sound on iPhone:* tap the orb once — iOS requires a tap before audio can play.
   - *Account won't connect:* sign out and back in, then retry from Social Accounts; make sure you approve all requested permissions.
   - *Numbers look stale:* data refreshes every few minutes; pull-to-refresh or reopen the Dashboard tab.
3. **Contact support** — email `support@makunto.app` with your account email and a short description. Screenshots help.
4. **Data requests** — export or delete your data anytime: Settings → Privacy, or email with subject "Data request".
