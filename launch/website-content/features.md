# Makunto Myra — Product Feature Descriptions

Ready-to-paste copy for the Horizon website. Each feature has a short card version and a longer section version.

## Tagline & one-liners
- **Tagline:** Your voice-first AI growth copilot.
- **One-liner:** Myra watches your channels, explains what changed and why, and tells you what to do next — out loud.
- **Sub-headline:** Talk to your analytics. Makunto Myra turns YouTube, Instagram and Facebook data into a daily conversation about growing your audience.

## Core features

### 1. Talk to Myra (voice-first AI)
**Card:** Ask "how's my channel doing?" and hear a real answer — grounded in your live numbers, spoken back in seconds.
**Section:** Myra is a spoken conversation, not a dashboard. Say the wake word, ask anything about your channels — subscribers, views, what worked, what didn't — and she answers with your actual, current numbers and the reason behind every change. She remembers past conversations, so "what changed since last time?" really works.

### 2. Live multi-platform analytics
**Card:** YouTube, Instagram and Facebook in one place — subscribers, views, watch time and daily changes.
**Section:** Connect your accounts once and Myra tracks everything: totals, day-over-day deltas, recent uploads, private YouTube analytics (watch time, retention, traffic sources). Ask about one platform or say "how's everything?" for a spoken tour of your whole presence.

### 3. Daily AI Brief
**Card:** A seven-part morning report: health, competitors, trends, today's opportunity, warnings, when to upload, and one quick win.
**Section:** Every morning Myra builds a brief from your fresh numbers: how healthy the channel is, what competitors did, which trend matters to you, the single best opportunity today, anything to worry about, the ideal upload window, and a two-minute quick win.

### 4. Competitor tracking
**Card:** Add any channel as a competitor and Myra tells you when they surge — and why.
**Section:** Track competitor channels alongside your own. Myra compares growth rates, spots the formats fueling their rise, and tells you where you're gaining or losing ground — in plain spoken language, not spreadsheets.

### 5. Trend Radar
**Card:** What's blowing up right now, filtered through what's relevant to *your* content.
**Section:** The Radar tab surfaces trending videos and formats. Ask Myra "what's trending for me?" and she connects the trend to your niche with a concrete video idea.

### 6. Session memory
**Card:** Myra remembers what you talked about — pick up any conversation where you left off.
**Section:** Every conversation is saved. Myra recaps what changed since you last spoke and references earlier advice, like a strategist who actually remembers your last meeting.

### 7. Premium voices & personalities
**Card:** Choose Myra's voice and personality — friendly, professional, hype, or calm.
**Section:** Pick from device voices (free) or premium AI voices. Set the personality that fits you: a polished consultant, an energetic hype friend, or a calm coach. Same intelligence, your vibe.

### 8. Private & secure
**Card:** Your data stays yours. Sign-in protected, OAuth-connected, never sold.
**Section:** Accounts connect through official platform OAuth — Myra never sees your passwords. Personal analytics are locked to your signed-in account, and you can disconnect or delete everything at any time.

## Feature bullets (compact list for hero/footer)
- Voice-first: ask, don't dig
- Live YouTube, Instagram & Facebook analytics
- Daily 7-part AI brief
- Competitor tracking with "why" explanations
- Trend Radar with personalized ideas
- Conversation memory across sessions
- Custom voices & personalities
- OAuth-secured, privacy-first
