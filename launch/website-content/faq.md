# FAQ Content

Matches the in-app FAQ at `/legal.html#faq`. Ready for a website accordion component.

**What is Makunto Myra?**
Myra is a voice-first AI copilot for content creators. She connects to your social accounts, watches your numbers, and answers questions out loud — with the reasons behind every change and clear advice on what to do next.

**Which platforms does Myra support?**
YouTube (including private analytics), Instagram and Facebook today. X, TikTok, LinkedIn, Threads and Pinterest are coming soon.

**Do I need to type, or can I just talk?**
Just talk. Say the wake word or tap the orb, ask your question, and Myra speaks the answer. Everything also appears as text on screen.

**Is my data safe?**
Yes. Accounts connect through each platform's official OAuth — Myra never sees your passwords. Your analytics are locked to your signed-in account, encrypted in transit, and never sold. You can disconnect any account or delete your data at any time.

**Does Myra post or change anything on my accounts?**
No. Myra is read-only — she analyzes and advises. Creating and publishing content is what Makunto Studio (a separate product) is for.

**How does the Daily Brief work?**
Each morning Myra builds a seven-part report from your fresh numbers: channel health, competitor moves, a relevant trend, today's opportunity, any warnings, the best upload window, and one quick win.

**Can I track competitors?**
Yes — add any public channel as a competitor. Myra compares their growth to yours and tells you what's driving it.

**Do I need a big channel for this to be useful?**
No. If you're just starting, Myra switches into mentor mode: helping you pick a niche, name your channel, plan your first videos, and build a posting habit.

**How much does it cost?**
See the current pricing on the website. A free tier lets you talk to Myra and explore; connecting accounts and personal analytics require an account.

**Can Myra speak in different voices?**
Yes — free device voices, or premium AI voices, plus selectable personalities (friendly, professional, hype, calm).

**What happens if I disconnect a platform?**
Tracking stops immediately and you can request deletion of the stored history for that platform from Settings.

**Who do I contact for help?**
Email support (see the Contact section) or use Help & FAQ inside the app.
