## Long Description

**Meet Myra — the AI companion that actually talks with you about your content career.**

Stop staring at dashboards. Just ask.

"How's my channel doing?" — Myra checks your real YouTube, Instagram and Facebook analytics and tells you, out loud, in plain language.

**WHAT MYRA DOES**
• **Voice-first analytics** — subscribers, views, watch time and engagement, spoken naturally. No charts to decode.
• **Daily Briefing** — every day Myra prepares a spoken summary of what changed and what to do about it.
• **Competitor Radar** — track competitor channels and trending topics in your niche, and hear what's working for them.
• **Growth coaching** — Myra remembers your goals and past conversations, and mentors you like a strategist who knows your channel.
• **Beginner friendly** — no channel yet? Myra interviews you, helps you find your niche, and walks you through starting.

**BUILT FOR CREATORS**
YouTube, Instagram and Facebook today — X, TikTok, LinkedIn, Threads and Pinterest coming soon.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
Read-only access through official APIs. Myra can never post, edit or delete anything. Your data is never sold or used to train AI.

Your channels. Your voice. Your companion.
**Makunto Myra.**
